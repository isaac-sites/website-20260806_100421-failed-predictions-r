## Introduction

[Dorothy Martin]({{ 'dorothy-martin/' | relative_url }})'s 1954 prediction that flying saucers would rescue believers before a catastrophic flood is often remembered as a failed UFO prophecy that inspired *When Prophecy Fails*. Less often examined is how the prediction changed once it became public. Before the appointed date arrived, newspaper coverage, persistent telephone calls, practical jokes and the presence of outsiders altered the social environment in which believers made decisions. Rather than observing a sealed religious group, journalists, pranksters and eventually even researchers became part of the unfolding event. This matters because the historical record of Martin's failed prediction reflects not only the group's beliefs but also the effects of intense public attention and outside [interference]({{ 'interference/' | relative_url }}).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jhbs.70043" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: onlinelibrary.wiley.com">[Wiley Online Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">onlinelibrary.wiley.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Online Library Debunking “When Prophecy Fails”</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wiley Online LibraryDebunking “When Prophecy Fails” - Kelly - 2026 - Journal of the History of the Behavioral Sciences - Wiley Online Lib...</span></span></span>

## How the prediction first reached the press

Dorothy Martin's circle was relatively small before local reporting drew broader attention to its prediction of a December 1954 rescue by extraterrestrials. The unusual combination of an exact deadline, an impending catastrophe and [the promise]({{ 'the-promise/' | relative_url }}) of a spacecraft landing made the story inherently newsworthy. Once newspapers published the forecast, curiosity spread well beyond committed believers.

Media attention had immediate practical consequences. Reporters telephoned Martin's home seeking interviews and confirmation of details. Curious members of the public also contacted the group, while sceptics and pranksters recognised an opportunity to interfere. Instead of awaiting the deadline in relative isolation, believers found themselves responding to constant external demands for information and reacting to messages from people whose motives ranged from genuine curiosity to ridicule.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jhbs.70043" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: onlinelibrary.wiley.com">[Wiley Online Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">onlinelibrary.wiley.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Online Library Debunking “When Prophecy Fails”</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wiley Online LibraryDebunking “When Prophecy Fails” - Kelly - 2026 - Journal of the History of the Behavioral Sciences - Wiley Online Lib...</span></span></span>

This transformation is significant because it converted what might otherwise have remained an internal prophetic test into a public performance. Every new article increased the number of people watching the countdown, while every telephone call introduced another possible source of influence.

## Prank calls, reporters and false rescue messages

One of the clearest examples of media-driven interference came several days before the predicted rescue. According to the original field notes later published in *When Prophecy Fails*, Martin received a telephone call from someone identifying himself as "Captain Video" from outer space. The caller announced that a flying saucer would arrive that afternoon to collect the believers.

Although the researchers regarded the caller as a practical joker responding to newspaper publicity, the message briefly affected the group's behaviour. Members debated whether it might be authentic, eventually accepted the possibility, removed metallic objects from their clothing in preparation for boarding the spacecraft, and waited for the promised arrival. When nothing happened, the failed rescue was not immediately treated as a complete disconfirmation. Instead, some members interpreted it as a rehearsal or preliminary test before the main prophetic deadline.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/When_Prophecy_Fails" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">When Prophecy Fails</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">When Prophecy Fails</span></span></span>

The episode illustrates several mechanisms through which outsiders reshaped events:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--decision" markdown="1">

* **Hoax information became part of believers' decision-making.** The group did not simply ignore unsolicited messages; at least temporarily, they incorporated them into their expectations.
* **Publicity created new opportunities for deception.** Without newspaper attention, prank callers would probably never have known whom to contact or what claims would appear plausible.
* **Observers unintentionally changed the phenomenon they hoped to witness.** Each unexpected message forced believers to reinterpret events before the official deadline had even arrived.

</div>

Reporters themselves also became active participants simply through their continued presence. Telephone enquiries, requests for interviews and public anticipation increased the pressure surrounding each predicted milestone. Instead of privately evaluating prophetic messages, Martin's followers did so while aware that journalists were waiting to report success or failure.

## Why observer interference complicates the historical record

The role of outsiders has become even more important in recent reassessments of the case. The traditional interpretation, derived largely from *When Prophecy Fails*, presents the researchers as relatively passive observers documenting how believers responded after the prophecy failed.

More recent archival research challenges that picture. Historian Thomas Kelly argues that the investigators themselves were considerably less detached than later readers assumed. Drawing on newly available archival material, he contends that researchers engaged in forms of covert participation, including deceptive interactions and interventions that affected the group's experience. Kelly also argues that publicity and recruitment activity existed before the prophecy failed, contradicting the long-standing claim that evangelising began only afterwards.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jhbs.70043" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: onlinelibrary.wiley.com">[Wiley Online Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">onlinelibrary.wiley.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Online Library Debunking “When Prophecy Fails”</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wiley Online LibraryDebunking “When Prophecy Fails” - Kelly - 2026 - Journal of the History of the Behavioral Sciences - Wiley Online Lib...</span></span></span>

These findings do not eliminate the importance of the original observations, but they complicate how historians interpret them. If prank callers, reporters and even some researchers influenced conversations, expectations or behaviour, then the surviving record documents an interaction between believers and outsiders rather than an untouched example of prophetic disappointment.

## The deadline became a public event rather than a private prophecy

By the time the predicted rescue date arrived, Dorothy Martin's deadline had become something larger than an internal religious expectation. Media coverage had produced an audience. Practical jokers had inserted false messages into the group's communications. Reporters had transformed each passing hour into a news event whose outcome would immediately become public knowledge.

This shift helps explain why the episode occupies such a prominent place in discussions of failed UFO predictions. It was not merely a prophecy that failed; it was a prophecy whose public visibility altered the conditions under which believers experienced that failure. The interaction between faith, journalism and public curiosity became part of the event itself, making it more historically significant but also more difficult to interpret as a simple, isolated case of prophetic belief.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jhbs.70043" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: onlinelibrary.wiley.com">[Wiley Online Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">onlinelibrary.wiley.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Online Library Debunking “When Prophecy Fails”</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wiley Online LibraryDebunking “When Prophecy Fails” - Kelly - 2026 - Journal of the History of the Behavioral Sciences - Wiley Online Lib...</span></span></span>

    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
